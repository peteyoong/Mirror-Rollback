- **Lenses Update 2 (Personal Snapshot):** For each Lens, add a personalized Your Snapshot section generated by an AI, and provide a more structured Deep Dive for Human Design.
- **Lens Chatbot:** Add a persistent, per-lens chatbot to the bottom of each Lens Deep Dive page.
